An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Describe Your Ex in One Word"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-describe-your-ex-in-one-word-9fc6ebe7): sum up an ex in exactly one word, at most 111 characters, nothing else attached.

## The word

**Unsubscribe**

Some exes are not a heartbreak — they are a subscription you forgot you signed up for, quietly charging you every month until you finally find the cancel button. One word, and the joke lands on its own.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single word to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. Nothing else is written, no extra text, no punctuation.

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
